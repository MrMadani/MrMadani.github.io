01 About

My career sits where three worlds meet: security, sales and automation.

For nearly six years I was the network and security engineer for a 100+ user manufacturing company, responsible for its firewalls, endpoints, identity, email security, AWS and audits. That taught me to think in systems, spot risk early and keep things running under pressure.

Today I'm Marketing and Key Accounts Coordinator at ImprintID, a promotional products supplier, working across marketing, key accounts, sales support and business development, where I handle our VIP distributor accounts. I bring the same discipline from security to the customer side: clear processes, reliable follow-through and data you can trust.

Alongside that, my team and I help businesses automate their workflows with AI, so people spend their time on customers instead of copy-paste.
