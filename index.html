01 About

My career sits where three worlds meet: security, sales and automation.

I started in cybersecurity engineering, working on SIEM, vulnerability management, network security and cloud security. That taught me to think in systems, spot risk early and keep things running under pressure.

Today I work at ImprintID, a promotional products supplier, across marketing, key accounts, sales support and business development, where I manage relationships with our VIP distributors. I bring the same discipline from security to the customer side: clear processes, reliable follow-through and data you can trust.

Alongside that, my team and I help businesses automate their workflows with AI, so people spend their time on customers instead of copy-paste.
